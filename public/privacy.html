Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026

The short version
  • We collect only what the game needs: your sign-in details, your player name and your game progress.
  • We don't sell your data, we don't show ads and we don't use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Other players can see your player name, your hero and your rank and results. They never see your email address.
  • You can ask us to see, correct, export or delete your data at any time.

1. Who we are

Atomic Bunch (the game at atomicbunch.com, "the Game") is developed and run by an independent game developer based in Spain ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Spanish data protection law (LOPDGDD).

You can contact us about anything in this policy, including requests to use your rights, by sending us an email.

3. What other players can see

Your player name, the hero and cards you play in a match, your rank, and your monthly wins, losses and ratio on the Legendary leaderboard are visible to other players. Your email address is stored separately and is never shown to other players.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

The Game stores a few things in your browser so that it works: your sign-in session, your language and display settings, and your tutorial progress. Google reCAPTCHA (used by Firebase App Check) may also set a cookie to tell real players apart from bots. These are all strictly necessary for the service you asked for, so they don't need your consent.

We don't currently use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. If we ever add them, we will ask for your consent first and you can play the Game whether you agree or not.

5. Who processes your data for us

We use a small number of service providers. They may process your data only on our instructions and must keep it secure:

  • Google (Firebase): sign-in, database, server functions and App Check with reCAPTCHA. Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC.
  • Google Fonts: some lettering in the Game is loaded from Google's servers, which receive your IP address when the font loads.
  • Website hosting: GitHub, Inc. (Microsoft) hosts the Game's files today. We may move hosting to Cloudflare, Inc. Hosting providers process your IP address and basic request information to deliver the website and protect it from attacks.
  • Payments: Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe Limited and Stripe, Inc.), only if and when we offer purchases.

We don't sell your personal data, and we don't share it with anyone else except where the law requires us to (for example a valid order from a court or authority).

6. Transfers outside the EU

Some of these providers process data in the United States. Google, Microsoft (GitHub), Cloudflare and Stripe are certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and their contracts also include the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. These safeguards are designed to give your data a level of protection equivalent to the EU's.

7. How long we keep your data

  • Registered accounts: for as long as your account exists. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account and game data within 30 days.
  • Guest accounts: deleted automatically, together with the guest name, 30 days after the guest game.
  • Finished matches: deleted automatically 30 days after the match.
  • Queue entries while you search for an opponent: deleted when the match starts or within a day at the latest.
  • Purchase records: kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires (in Spain generally up to 6 years).
  • Emails to us: kept as long as needed to deal with your request, and then for up to 2 years in case of follow-up questions.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • get a copy of your personal data and information about how we use it (access);
  • have incorrect data corrected (rectification);
  • have your data deleted (erasure);
  • restrict how we use your data;
  • receive the data you gave us in a structured, machine-readable format (portability);
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw any consent you have given, at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

To use any of these rights, email us from the address linked to your account (or tell us your player name). We reply within one month. Using your rights is free.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, for example the authority in the EU country where you live, or the Spanish Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es). We'd appreciate the chance to sort things out with you first.

If you signed in with Google, you can also remove the Game's access to your Google account at any time in your Google account settings.

9. Players in the United States

We don't sell personal information and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined in the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA) and similar U.S. state laws. The categories of data we collect, where they come from, why we use them and who receives them are described in sections 2 to 5. Wherever you live, you can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it, using the email link in section 8, and we will not treat you differently for doing so.

10. Children

The Game is not meant for children. You must be at least 16 years old to create an account or play. We don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16, and in the United States the Game is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal data, tell us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as hashes by our sign-in provider. Game progress and currency can only be changed by our server functions, and email addresses are kept in a separate area that other players can't read. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will inform you and the authorities of a data breach where the law requires it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game or the law changes. The date at the top always shows the current version. If a change significantly affects how we use your data, we will tell you in the Game before it takes effect.
